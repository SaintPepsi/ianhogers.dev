I help out with the Kingston Farmers Market at Kingston Heath Reserve. On market mornings, getting every stallholder into the right spot used to run off a printed list covered in ticks, crossings-out and arrows.

So I built a little page for it: zones and spots across the reserve, who goes where, who's arrived, who's paid, and a printable sheet for when paper is still easier.
